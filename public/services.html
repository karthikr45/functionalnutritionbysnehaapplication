Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which service is right for me?

Start with a free 30-minute discovery call. We'll discuss your goals, challenges, and budget to determine which service will give you the best results. You can book through the contact page.

Are sessions conducted online or in-person?

Both options are available! I offer sessions via Zoom, Google Meet, or phone for clients worldwide, as well as in-person sessions in Mumbai, India. Many clients prefer the convenience of online sessions.

What conditions do you specialize in?

I specialize in gut disorders (IBS, SIBO, leaky gut), hormonal imbalances, thyroid conditions, diabetes and blood sugar issues, autoimmune conditions, chronic fatigue, weight management, and general wellness optimization.

How soon will I see results?

Many clients notice improved energy and digestion within 2–4 weeks. Significant, lasting changes typically happen over 3–6 months depending on the complexity of your health situation and your commitment to the program.

Do you accept insurance?

Currently, I do not work directly with insurance providers. However, I provide detailed invoices and receipts that many clients successfully submit for HSA/FSA reimbursement. I also offer payment plans to make services accessible.

Can you work with my doctor or other healthcare providers?

Absolutely! I strongly believe in a collaborative, integrative approach. With your permission, I can coordinate with your physician, endocrinologist, or other specialists to ensure a comprehensive care plan.
