My Journey

From Personal Struggle to Purpose-Driven Practice

My passion for functional nutrition began with my own health journey. After years of battling chronic fatigue, hormonal imbalances, and digestive issues, I discovered that food truly is medicine.

Conventional medicine offered me temporary solutions, but it was functional nutrition that gave me lasting answers. I spent years studying the science of how food interacts with our bodies at a cellular level — and the results were life-changing.

Today, I bring that same transformative approach to my clients. My mission is to help you uncover the root causes of your health challenges, restore balance, and build a vibrant life you love.

Root cause focus, not symptom suppression
Evidence-based, personalized approach
Compassionate, judgment-free support
Sustainable lifestyle changes, not quick fixes

How I Work

My Approach to Your Health

A structured, systematic process to help you achieve lasting transformation.

01

Deep Health Assessment

We start with a comprehensive intake covering your health history, symptoms, diet, lifestyle, sleep, stress, and any lab results. This 360-degree view forms the foundation of your personalized plan.

02

Root Cause Investigation

Using the functional medicine matrix, we identify the underlying imbalances — whether in your gut, hormones, inflammation, detox, or other systems — that are driving your health issues.

03

Personalized Nutrition Plan

You receive a customized eating plan designed for your unique biochemistry, health goals, food preferences, and lifestyle — not a generic diet template.

04

Implementation & Support

Through regular check-ins, coaching calls, and ongoing adjustments, we ensure you stay on track and continue progressing toward your goals with confidence.

05

Long-Term Optimization

We track your progress with measurable outcomes and refine your plan as your body responds and evolves, ensuring you maintain your gains and continue to thrive.
